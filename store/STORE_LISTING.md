Magic Coloring World is a joyful, ad-free creative playground made for children aged 3–8.
Every picture, sound and character is 100% original — no copyrighted content — and the whole
app follows the Google Play Families Policy.

🎨 COLORING
• 120+ original coloring pages: animals, dinosaurs, cars, princesses, space, sea life, farm,
  birds, flowers, food — plus big detailed scenes (gardens, houses by the sea, farms and more).

• Bucket fill, unlimited undo/redo, zoom, rotate, mirror & grid drawing.
• 64 colors plus pastel, neon, metallic, glitter & rainbow palettes.
• "Color by Numbers" mode that teaches numbers while coloring.
• A sample picture and a live progress bar so kids can copy and finish with confidence.

🧸 A TALKING BUDDY

🎮 FUN LEARNING GAMES
• Balloon Pop (colors, letters, numbers, animals) with high scores.
• Connect the Dots — 100 puzzles.
• Shadow Match — match pictures to their shadows.
• Learn & Say cards: animals, colors, shapes, ABC, numbers, vehicles, fruits and more.

🏆 REWARDS
• Stars, coins, a daily treasure chest, surprise chests and 16 collectible trophies.

👨‍👩‍👧 MADE FOR FAMILIES
• No third-party ads. No behavioural tracking.
• Parental gate protects all settings and links.
• Multiple kid profiles, left-handed mode, large-button mode, colour-blind palette.
• Sleep timer, 9 languages and 4 voice characters.
• Works offline.

Download Magic Coloring World and watch your little artist create, learn and smile! 🌈
